# Training and Testing

In class we split students at random into 80% train and 20% test. That would be cheating here, because permits filed in the same year share the same conditions. We split by time instead: train on projects filed through 2018, using only what was known on January 1, 2019, and test on projects filed in 2019, 2020 and 2021. A project still waiting on the cutoff date counts as "still waiting", not as a failure. Every site stays on one side of the split.
